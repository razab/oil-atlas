                                                                                                Management System as per
                                                                                                ISO 50001 : 2018




Following the successful completion of our expansion and modernization
project, achieving ISO certifications became a strategic priority to align our
enhanced capabilities with global standards. We recognized that our upgraded
infrastructure required an equally robust management framework to ensure
our modernized units operate responsibly.

Environmental Responsibility (ISO 14001) Through ISO 14001, we have
systematized our approach to environmental protection. This standard enables
us to manage our ecological impact by optimizing resource efficiency and
                                                                                                 Targeted to
                                                                                                 be achieved
minimizing waste, ensuring that our expanded operations remain
environmentally sustainable.

Occupational Health and Safety (ISO 45001) With ISO 45001, we have
formalized our commitment to the well-being of our workforce. This
framework shifts our focus from reactive compliance to proactive risk
prevention, creating a safety culture where hazard elimination and employee
health are the foundation of our daily operations.
                                                                                                 in 2026
Quality Management (ISO 9001) Our pursuit of operational excellence is
underpinned by ISO 9001, the global benchmark for quality management. This
certification ensures that our expanded production capacities are matched by
consistent product quality and streamlined processes. It anchors our
commitment to customer satisfaction, ensuring that every product leaving our
facility meets rigorous international standards.

Laboratory Competence (ISO 17025) To validate the integrity of our
products, our laboratories maintain ISO 17025 accreditation. This standard
confirms the technical competence and precision of our testing facilities. It
ensures that the analytical data driving our decision-making and product
certification is accurate, unbiased, and recognized globally, reinforcing trust in
our quality control.
